## Critical anti-patterns

| Pattern | Description | Severity | Prevention mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frontend assumes latest companion schema | This caused `scan.host.operatingSystem` to throw and the page to turn black. | blocking | Route every scan through `normalizeScan`; browser-test with the old running companion before deploy. |
| Copy explains the product instead of showing value | Six descriptive cards pushed inventory and findings below the fold. | advisory | Keep the new action-first dashboard; remove explanatory cards and long paragraphs. |
| Overclaiming availability | A config entry does not prove the IDE loaded, trusted, or started an MCP. | blocking | Use “configured” and expose policy/command evidence separately. |
